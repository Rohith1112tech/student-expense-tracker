# Project Statement

## Project Title

Student Expense Tracker & Budget Analyzer

## Problem Statement

Students frequently spend money on food, transport, educational materials, subscriptions, entertainment, and other daily activities. When these transactions are not recorded systematically, students may not know how much they spend in each category or whether their monthly spending is within a planned budget.

The proposed system solves this problem by providing a lightweight command-line application for recording income and expenses, managing monthly budgets, analyzing spending categories, and generating reports.

## Scope of the Project

The project focuses on personal financial record keeping for students. It supports:

- Recording income
- Recording expenses
- Viewing and deleting transactions
- Maintaining monthly budgets
- Calculating total income and expenses
- Finding category-wise spending
- Checking budget utilization
- Generating monthly reports
- Persisting data in JSON format

The project does not connect to bank accounts or process real financial transactions.

## Target Users

The primary target users are:

- College students
- Students living away from home
- Students who receive a fixed monthly allowance
- Students who want to understand their spending patterns

## High-Level Features

1. Transaction management
2. Monthly budget management
3. Expense analytics
4. Monthly reporting
5. Local JSON storage
6. Input validation
7. Error handling
8. Automated unit testing

## Expected Outcome

The expected outcome is a reliable command-line application that allows a student to maintain a simple personal financial record and obtain useful summaries from the stored information.
